Attendance Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

1. Introduction

Attendance Tracker (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) is a school attendance management application used by authorized school staff to record student attendance, manage leave documents, and request approval for edits to previous-day attendance records. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

2. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to:

  • Teachers and school staff who use the App
  • Attendance approvers (Attendance In-charge, HOD, Vice Principal, Principal, Admin)
  • School administrators who manage classes, students, and access

3. Information we collect

Depending on your role and usage, we may process:

  • Account information: name, email, role, login credentials (stored securely), and phone number used for WhatsApp approval notifications
  • Student information: student name, roll number, class/section, and attendance status (Present, Absent, Late, Half Day, and related statuses)
  • Attendance records: date, class/section, marked status, and who submitted or edited the record
  • Edit approval data: edit request reason, request status (Pending/Approved/Denied), approver response, and related timestamps
  • Documents: leave letters or other school documents uploaded by authorized users
  • Technical logs: basic server logs needed for security, debugging, and service reliability

4. How we use information

We use information only to operate school attendance workflows, including to:

  • Authenticate users and manage role-based access
  • Record, display, and report student attendance
  • Lock previous-day attendance and manage edit-permission requests
  • Send WhatsApp approval messages to assigned approvers when a teacher requests permission to edit past attendance
  • Process Approve/Deny responses received through WhatsApp webhooks
  • Store leave documents and related school records for authorized school use
  • Maintain audit trails for attendance edits and approvals

5. WhatsApp messaging

The App uses Meta WhatsApp Cloud API only for operational school notifications related to attendance edit approvals. When a teacher submits an edit request for a previous attendance date, we may send a WhatsApp message to the assigned approver containing:

  • Teacher name
  • Class and section
  • Attendance date
  • Reason for the edit request
  • Approve / Deny action buttons

We do not use WhatsApp for marketing, advertising, spam, or unsolicited promotional messages. Messages are sent only as part of an authenticated staff-initiated approval workflow.

6. Legal basis / purpose of processing

We process information to provide the school attendance service requested by the school, to support legitimate educational and administrative operations, and to keep attendance records accurate and auditable.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal data. Information may be shared only with:

  • Authorized school staff based on their role in the App
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Cloud API) solely to deliver approval messages and receive button responses for the edit-approval workflow
  • Hosting / infrastructure providers required to run the App, under appropriate access controls
  • Authorities if required by applicable law

8. Data retention

Attendance records, edit requests, audit logs, and uploaded documents are retained as needed for school operations and record-keeping. Schools may request correction or deletion of data where appropriate and legally permitted.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, including authenticated access (JWT), role-based authorization, server-side credential storage, and restricted access to WhatsApp API credentials. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10. Children’s data

The App processes student attendance information for school administrative purposes. Access is limited to authorized school personnel. The App is not intended for use by children as end users.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law and school policy, you may request to:

  • Access personal information held about you in the App
  • Correct inaccurate account or contact information
  • Update or remove a WhatsApp phone number used for approvals
  • Ask for deletion of certain records where permitted

Staff users should contact their school administrator first. School administrators may contact us using the details below.

12. Third-party services

WhatsApp messaging is provided through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform. Use of WhatsApp is also subject to Meta’s terms and policies.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made. Continued use of the App after an update means the revised policy applies.
